## Slide 1 — Title

> **Wasalni**
> *Ride-hailing for the cities Uber forgot.*

Visual: hero shot of a Tuk-Tuk waiting in front of an Egyptian mosque at sunset.

---

## Slide 2 — The Forgotten Market

- 70% of Egyptians live outside Cairo and Alexandria.
- 200+ secondary cities, each with 50K-2M residents.
- Uber and Careem cover ~15 cities. **Zero** of the rest.
- The transport gap: people walking, hailing informal taxis, or stuck.

Visual: map of Egypt with Uber-served cities lit up vs. the dark majority.

---

## Slide 3 — The Vehicle Reality

- Tuk-Tuks dominate short urban + village trips (5-12 km).
- Motorcycles dominate young-rider trips (3-6 km).
- Uber and Careem refuse to support them. Their global product doesn't fit.
- These are **60-80% of trips in secondary cities**.

Visual: side-by-side photo of an Uber screen (no Tuk-Tuk option) vs Wasalni (full vehicle selector).

---

## Slide 4 — Our Solution

A purpose-built ride-hailing platform, white-labeled per city:

- Full Tuk-Tuk + Motorcycle support.
- Cash-first payments + Egyptian mobile wallets.
- Arabic RTL + Ramadan-aware fare engine.
- Female-driver-only mode.
- OpenStreetMap-based to avoid Google's costs.
- Production-tested in Bagour, El Menofia.

Visual: 4-screen mockup (passenger / driver / admin / live map).

---

## Slide 5 — Business Model: Sell, Don't Operate

We don't operate cities. We **license the platform** to local operators.

Tier prices: EGP 150K - 3.5M one-time + 25K-300K/yr support.

Why this beats operating directly:
- Asset-light: no fleet, no drivers, no operations.
- Repeatable: same product, sold ~200 times.
- High margin: ~70% gross margin on each license.
- Risk-distributed: bad cities don't sink us.
- Scalable: software economics + Egypt manufacturing labor cost for support.

Visual: revenue waterfall — one tier sold per month → ARR growth.

## Slide 9 — Competition

- **Uber / Careem**: not coming. Wrong product, wrong economics.
- **Indrive**: bidding model, no local ownership angle.
- **Generic SaaS**: wrong vertical, wrong language, no Tuk-Tuk.
- **Build from scratch**: 18 months + EGP 2M, high failure rate.

We're the **only** purpose-built licensing platform for this market.

---

## Slide 10 — The Moat

What competitors can't easily copy:

1. **Tuk-Tuk + Motorcycle native support** (architectural).
2. **Egyptian payment integrations** (3-6 months work).
3. **Arabic UX + cultural awareness** (Ramadan mode, female-only, etc.).
4. **Cash workflow** (commission tracking, driver wallet settlement).
5. **OSM cost structure** (10x cheaper to run than Google Maps).
6. **Operator playbook** (we know how to launch).
7. **Reference customers** in Bagour, El Menofia (proof points).
